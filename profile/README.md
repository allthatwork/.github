## Show all that work.

All That Work is a workspace for running a serious job search. Keep the facts and stories behind your career, turn them into resumes and cover letters, and carry that context through applications and interviews.

### From experience to the next opportunity

- **Keep your career knowledge.** Save accomplishments, skills, and interview stories so you can reuse them across your job search.
- **Build the documents.** Write and tailor resumes and cover letters, review proposed AI edits, and export your work.
- **Follow each application.** Keep job postings, contacts, notes, follow-ups, and the documents you sent together.
- **Prepare for the conversation.** Work through interview questions and practice answers grounded in your own experience.
- **Choose your AI.** Connect your preferred provider, control what context it receives, and adjust context budgets and time limits.

### Where the project stands

All That Work is independently maintained and actively developed as a self-hosted application, built on [Reactive Resume](https://github.com/reactive-resume/reactive-resume). A hosted product is in development; public signups are not open yet.

This GitHub organization is the project's development home. Membership is by invitation. You can browse the source, self-host the application, or report an issue without joining the organization.
